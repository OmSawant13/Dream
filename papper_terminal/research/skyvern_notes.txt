# SKYVERN: INDUSTRIAL RELIABILITY & ARCHITECTURE
(Deep Research Notes)

## 1. THE PERCEPTION ENGINE (Vision-First)
- **Logic:** Skyvern treats the browser as a 'Vision Task'.
- **Algorithm:** It overlays the AXTree on top of a screenshot to find exact (X, Y) coordinates. 
- **Secret:** It uses a 'Token Budget Fallback'. If the DOM is too large, it prunes elements that are 'hidden', 'occluded', or 'semantically empty' until the context fits the LLM.

## 2. REASONING & PLANNING
- **Speculative Execution:** The agent maintains a 'Hypothesis' of the next 3 steps. If the page state matches the hypothesis, it executes immediately without re-planning.
- **Workflow Persistence:** It can 'record' a workflow and replay it by matching current DOM states to previously recorded states.

## 3. FAILURE CLASSIFIER (Self-Correction)
- **Algorithm:** Skyvern doesn't just 'try again'. It classifies failures into:
  - **Site Error:** The website is down or 404.
  - **Agent Hallucination:** The agent tried to click something that isn't there.
  - **Bot Blocked:** Detection triggered.
- **Recovery:** Based on the classification, it either refreshes, re-plans, or asks the human for help.

## 4. UNIQUE CAPABILITIES
- **OTP Handling:** Native logic to wait for and extract verification codes from emails/SMS.
- **PDF Extraction:** Can 'read' PDFs directly from the browser's native viewer.
